
# Stock Market Summary & Financial Statement Analysis

## Part A: Stock Market Summary

### 1. What is a Stock Market?
A stock market is a marketplace where investors buy and sell shares of publicly listed companies.

### 2. NSE and BSE
- NSE: National Stock Exchange of India.
- BSE: Bombay Stock Exchange.
Both are recognised stock exchanges in India.

### 3. Nifty and Sensex
- Nifty 50 tracks 50 major companies listed on NSE.
- Sensex tracks 30 major companies listed on BSE.
They are important indicators of Indian stock market performance.

### 4. IPO and SME IPO
- IPO: Initial Public Offering, through which a company offers shares to the public for the first time.
- SME IPO: IPO conducted by eligible small and medium enterprises on dedicated SME platforms.

### 5. Market Capitalisation
Market Capitalisation = Share Price × Total Outstanding Shares.

It represents the total market value of a company's equity.

### 6. Important Financial Ratios and Terms
- PE Ratio: Share Price / Earnings Per Share.
- PB Ratio: Market Price per Share / Book Value per Share.
- EPS: Profit attributable to equity shareholders / Weighted Average Shares.
- Dividend: A portion of company profits distributed to shareholders.
- Bonus Shares: Additional shares issued to existing shareholders without additional payment.
- Stock Split: Division of existing shares into a larger number of shares, reducing face value proportionately.
- Trading Volume: Number of shares traded during a given period.

### 7. Company Financial Statements
- Balance Sheet: Shows assets, liabilities and shareholders' equity at a particular date.
- Profit and Loss Statement: Shows revenue, expenses and profit or loss over a period.
- Cash Flow Statement: Shows cash generated and used in operating, investing and financing activities.

### 8. Basic Financial Ratios
- Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities.
- Debt-to-Equity Ratio = Total Debt / Shareholders' Equity.
- Net Profit Margin = Net Profit / Revenue × 100.
- Operating Margin = Operating Profit / Revenue × 100.
- ROE = Net Profit / Average Shareholders' Equity × 100.

## Part B: Listed Company Financial Analysis

### 1. Company Profile

Company: Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Limited  
Former Name: Tata Motors Limited  
Industry: Automobile and Passenger Vehicles  
Reporting Period: FY 2025–26  
Analysis Basis: Consolidated Financial Statements  
Currency: Indian Rupees (₹ crore)

The company operates in passenger vehicles and has an international luxury vehicle business through Jaguar Land Rover (JLR).

### 2. Profit and Loss Analysis

| Particulars | FY26 (₹ crore) | FY25 (₹ crore) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Revenue from Operations | 3,35,582 | 3,66,094 |
| Operating Profit | 20,011 | 46,959 |
| Profit Before Tax | -1,623 | 28,454 |
| Profit After Tax | -1,377 | 19,394 |

**Observations:**

- Revenue declined by approximately 8.3%.
- Operating profit declined significantly.
- The company reported a loss before tax and a loss after tax from continuing operations.
- The financial performance was affected by US tariffs and a cyber incident at JLR that caused a five-week production pause.

### 3. Balance Sheet Analysis

The balance sheet provides information about the company's financial position.

Important indicators reported for FY26:

- Consolidated Net Debt: ₹30,710 crore.
- FY25 Consolidated Position: Net Cash of ₹1,018 crore.
- Domestic Operations Liquidity: ₹9,601 crore.
- JLR Liquidity: £6.9 billion, including an unutilised credit facility of £4.2 billion.

**Observation:**

The group moved from a net cash position to a net debt position during FY26. This indicates increased financial pressure and the importance of monitoring liquidity and borrowing levels.

### 4. Cash Flow Analysis

Important cash flow indicator:

- FY26 Free Cash Flow: Outflow of ₹26,823 crore.
- FY25 Free Cash Flow: Inflow of ₹22,236 crore.

**Observation:**

The negative free cash flow indicates that the group used more cash than it generated after relevant investments and other cash flow requirements. Production stoppages and working capital movements following the JLR cyber incident contributed to the pressure.

### 5. Basic Financial Ratio Analysis

**Revenue Growth**

Formula:

( Current Year Revenue - Previous Year Revenue ) / Previous Year Revenue × 100

Calculation:

(3,35,582 - 3,66,094) / 3,66,094 × 100

Result: Approximately -8.3%

**Operating Profit Margin**

Formula:

Operating Profit / Revenue × 100

Calculation:

20,011 / 3,35,582 × 100

Result: Approximately 5.96%

**Profit Before Tax Margin**

Formula:

Profit Before Tax / Revenue × 100

Result: Approximately -0.48%

**Profit After Tax Margin**

Formula:

Profit After Tax / Revenue × 100

Result: Approximately -0.41%

### 6. Overall Financial Performance

Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Limited experienced a challenging FY26.

Revenue declined, operating profitability weakened and the company reported losses from continuing operations. The JLR cyber incident and additional US tariffs were important factors affecting performance.

The company also experienced negative free cash flow and an increase in consolidated net debt.

### 7. Conclusion

Financial statement analysis helps Data Analysts understand a company's financial health, profitability, liquidity and operational performance.

This analysis demonstrates how revenue, profit margins, debt and cash flow indicators can be used to evaluate business performance.
